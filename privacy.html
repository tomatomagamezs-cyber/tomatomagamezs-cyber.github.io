Tomatoma Games privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers the games made by Tomatoma Games. Today that is Cake It Up, on the web and on Android. Contact: tomatoma.gamezs@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

It explains the gameplay stats our games send to us and the ads in our Android app. The website or store where you play has its own policy (see "Other companies" below). If we release another game that collects anything different, we update this page before it ships.

What we collect and why

Cake It Up sends gameplay stats to Tomatoma Games so we can find levels that are too hard, fix bugs and decide what to improve. We never ask for your name, email address or an account, and we do not sell this data or give it to advertisers.

Each stats event carries:

  • A random install ID. The game makes it on your device the first time you play. It is not your device ID, advertising ID or any account. It lets us count returning players without knowing who they are.
  • Session details. A random ID for each play session, the session number, when it started and how long it lasted, the time on your device when each event happened and the time we received it.
  • Game and device details. The game version, the platform (web or Android), the site or store the copy of the game is made for (CrazyGames, itch.io, GameDistribution, Google Play or a test build), whether it is a live or test build, your device language and region setting (for example en_US, which is how we estimate your country), the day you first played and how many days ago that was, and your stats and ad-consent choices.
  • What happens in the game. The first steps of a new game, progress milestones, levels started, won or lost (level number, attempt, moves, stars, time taken, boosters used, whether the board got stuck), boosters used and offers answered, coins earned and spent with the balance, daily streak changes, chests opened, ads offered, started, finished, skipped or failed (which ad spot, the reward, seconds watched), and short technical error codes.

Our stats do not include your name, email, contacts, photos, files, messages, precise location, advertising ID, device ID or anything you type. Our database does not store your IP address. Our hosting provider sees it briefly while delivering the data, as any web server does.

We do not use these stats to target ads.

For players in the EU, EEA and UK: we use these stats on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it. You can object at any time by turning stats off or by emailing us.

Ads in the Android app

The Android version of Cake It Up shows ads from Google AdMob: short videos you choose to watch for a reward, and full-screen ads between levels. The ads are served by Google, not by us.

To show and measure ads, Google's ad software in the app may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address, device and app details (such as the device model, operating system and app version) and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message before any ad is loaded, and Google uses your choice to decide which ads you see. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, Ad privacy choices.

We receive only ad totals from Google, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned. Google does not send us your advertising ID. Our own stats record which ad spot was offered or watched, as listed above, and your consent choice.

Where it is stored and for how long

Stats go over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our database at Supabase, hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Raw events are deleted after 13 months. We may keep daily totals (for example "players per day") that contain no install ID for longer.

On your device the game keeps your progress and settings, plus a short list of stats that have not been sent yet (at most 2,000). In a browser these live in the browser's storage for the game's site; when you hide or close the tab the game also keeps a copy of the unsent list in the browser's local storage, so it can send it next time. Sent stats are removed from the list. Your game progress is not sent to us.

Turning stats off

Open Settings and switch Share gameplay stats to Off. From then on:

  • the game sends nothing more;
  • the stats still waiting on your device are deleted, including the copy in your browser's local storage;
  • a batch that was already on its way at that exact moment may still arrive.

Switching it back on starts fresh and sends only new events. Turning stats off does not delete what we already received; to delete that, email us.

Deleting your data or getting a copy

Email tomatoma.gamezs@gmail.com with your install ID. You find it in the game under Settings, Privacy ("Your install ID"). We delete every event with that ID, or send you a copy, within 30 days and confirm by email. If you clear your browser data or reinstall the game, it makes a new install ID, so note the old one first. Without the install ID we cannot find your data, because we hold nothing else that identifies you.

You can also contact us with any other question or complaint about your data. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Other companies

The site or store where you play, and its advertising partners, collect data under their own privacy policies, not this one. When an ad partner needs your consent, it asks you itself.

  • CrazyGames: crazygames.com/privacy-policy
  • GameDistribution and the sites it supplies: static.gamedistribution.com/policy/privacy-enduser.html
  • itch.io: itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play and Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

We do not ask for names, contact details or anything else that identifies a player, child or adult. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a player's stats by email, as described above.

Changes

If we change what our games collect, we update this page and the date at the top before the new version ships.
